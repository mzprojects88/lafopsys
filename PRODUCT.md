# LAF Operating System (lafopsys)

**What it is.** The operations system of Little Ark Foundation's house for children under treatment and their carers: patients and admissions (the NCH house sheet, the floor plan, rides and pick-ups), staff time and HR, house operations, donors and receipts, finance, compliance deadlines, reports and the executive figures. Stock lives in the sibling app, LAF Inventory; lafopsys reads it.

**Who uses it, where.** Social workers and house staff on their own phones through the day: clocking in and out, checking patients in, the day's arrivals and rides. HR, finance and admins at a laptop: payroll, bank reconciliation, compliance, settings. The CEO and board read the executive figures. Donors use the portal.

**Mode.** Operate. Familiar admin conventions, dense enough for a month of records, nothing decorative.

**Constraints.**
- Phone and desk are both first-class: clock-in, check-in and the house sheet happen on phones; payroll and finance happen at a desk.
- Real data only. No sample rows, no placeholder names.
- Peso amounts with two decimals; dates as "9 Sep 2026"; Manila time.
- Every list is honest while loading: skeletons, never "₱0" or "Nothing yet" from data that has not arrived.
- Children's records are sensitive: show what the role needs, nothing more.
- Brand accent is LAF blue `#2563eb`, shared with LAF Inventory so both apps read as one product.
- Dark mode is a first-class theme.

**Voice.** Plain, short, the staff's own words: "Check in", "Clock out", "Pick-up", "House rules". Errors name the problem and the fix.
